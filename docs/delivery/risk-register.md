# Risk Register

**Scoring:** Likelihood (L) 1–5 × Impact (I) 1–5 = Score (S).
**Rating:** 1–6 Low · 8–12 Medium · 15–25 High.
**Status values:** Open · Mitigating · Monitoring · Closed.

The register is reviewed weekly by the Delivery Lead and PO, and the top 10 go to every Steering Committee. Owners are named roles; see [raci.md](raci.md) for the party codes.

## Risk review procedure

1. New risks can be raised by anyone through the delivery tracker using the template above.
2. The Delivery Lead triages within 2 business days, assigning an owner and an initial score.
3. Any risk scoring **High (≥ 15)** is escalated to the next Steering Committee with a mitigation plan.
4. A risk is closed only when its owner and the Delivery Lead agree that the cause no longer exists or the residual risk has been accepted by the accountable party in the RACI.
